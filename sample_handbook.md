# Aperture Robotics — Employee Handbook

## Leave

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per year. Unused leave may be
carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 6 days. Parental leave is 20 weeks at
full pay for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

Sick leave is uncapped but absences longer than 5 consecutive working days require a
medical certificate.

## Equipment

Every engineer receives a laptop, refreshed every 3 years. The home office stipend is
$1,200, claimable once every 2 years. Monitors and peripherals are ordered through the IT
portal and do not count against the stipend.

## Working arrangements

The company operates a hybrid model: a minimum of 2 days per week in the office, with
core collaboration hours between 10:00 and 16:00 local time. Fully remote arrangements
require director-level approval and are reviewed annually.

## Expenses

Expenses under $200 are approved automatically. Anything above $200 requires manager
sign-off before the spend. Reimbursements are paid with the following month's salary.
Travel booked less than 14 days in advance needs written justification.
